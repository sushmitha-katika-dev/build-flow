# Project Understanding

## Problem Statement
Small and medium-sized civil contractors currently manage their day-to-day business operations using notebooks, spreadsheets, phone calls, and messaging applications. Critical operational information (worker attendance, wages, material purchases, inventory, equipment usage, and expenses) is maintained manually across multiple scattered records. This leads to time-consuming calculations, inconsistent records, material wastage, budget overruns, and limited visibility into project profitability.

## Business Objective
To digitize and centralize daily business operations by developing **BuildFlow**, a Smart Business Operations Platform. The objective is to replace manual record-keeping with a unified digital system that integrates project management, workforce management, inventory management, equipment management, financial tracking, and business analytics. This will improve operational efficiency, enhance financial visibility, optimize resource utilization, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Scope
The scope of BuildFlow includes the development of the following core modules:
1. **Project Management:** Track project status, budgets, and actual expenditures.
2. **Workforce Management:** Manage labourer registration, daily attendance, and wage calculations.
3. **Material & Inventory Management:** Track material catalog (cement, steel, etc.), purchasing, consumption, and low-stock alerts.
4. **Equipment Management:** Manage equipment registry, allocation, fuel expenses, and maintenance.
5. **Financial Management:** Consolidate all project-wise expenses and track profitability.
6. **Reporting & Analytics:** Provide a dashboard for business owners to view active projects, stock status, and project profitability.

## Assumptions
- The application is tailored specifically for the workflows of small and medium-sized civil contractors, not large enterprise organizations.
- Site supervisors have access to mobile devices with internet connectivity to log daily attendance and material consumption in real-time.
- The business has standardized wage rates for labourers and a predefined catalog of commonly used construction materials.
- All financial tracking within the system is for internal operational monitoring (cost and profitability analysis) and does not replace dedicated corporate accounting software for tax purposes.
